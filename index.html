FAQ

Common questions

Do you provide home pickup?

Yes, local pickup can be arranged. Pickup and delivery charges depend on distance.

Can I pay after delivery?

Cash/UPI on delivery can be offered for local standard orders. Custom or higher-value orders may require advance payment.

Who provides the fabric?

For custom stitching, the customer usually provides the fabric. This keeps the service flexible and affordable.

How long does stitching take?

Delivery time depends on the garment and current workload. Confirm the date when the order is accepted.
